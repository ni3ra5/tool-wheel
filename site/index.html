    Features

    Your apps, one shortcut away

    1. 01

      Appears at your cursor

      Hold the shortcut and the wheel appears around your cursor, on whichever screen you're on.

    2. 02

      Designed like hardware

      Molded-plastic slices and a knob that turns in 10° detents, each with a soft, heavy thock.

    3. 03

      Two ways to launch

      Click a tool, or hover it and release the shortcut. Pick whichever you like in Settings.

    4. 04

      Shows running apps

      A small dot marks apps that are already running. Picking one brings its window forward instead of opening another copy.

    5. 05

      No permissions needed

      No accessibility prompts and no keyboard hooks. It only watches which modifier keys are held.

    6. 06

      Easy to customise

      Search your installed apps to add them, drag to reorder, trash to remove. It all lives in one tools.json.
